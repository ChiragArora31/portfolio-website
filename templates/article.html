ARTICLE TITLE

A precise one- or two-sentence explanation of what this essay investigates and why it matters.

Chirag Arora · month day, year · 8 min read

contents

  1. the problem
  2. the approach
  3. tradeoffs and failures
  4. what i learned

Open with the concrete situation that made this worth writing. Prefer an observation from the work over a broad claim about the industry.

the problem.

Describe the system, constraint, or failure mode precisely. Give the reader enough context to understand why the obvious solution was insufficient.

the approach.

Explain the architecture and the decisions you made. Use a short code sample when the implementation is more useful than another paragraph.

// Keep examples small enough to understand without horizontal archaeology.

tradeoffs and failures.

Write about what did not work, what surprised you, and which compromise you accepted. This is usually the most credible part of a technical essay.

Use callouts sparingly—for the sentence that should survive after the implementation details fade.

what i learned.

End with specific lessons, open questions, and what you would change in the next version.
